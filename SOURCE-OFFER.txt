VMware tools for Phorepoc -- licence notice and source offer

This package contains phevmwsvc.exe, a service that talks to the VMware host through the backdoor channel of the Phorepoc PC image
and shares the clipboard (text) between the host and the device. Everything in it is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public
License version 2.1 only (LGPL-2.1). The icon is our own artwork under the same licence. The licence text is installed with the
package in \resource\phevmw\licences\.

Corresponding source, available to anyone who has this package, for at least three years after the release you installed it from:

  1. Our files (os/src/phevmwsvc, registry, recipes) and the scripts that build and package the service:
       https://github.com/Phorepoc/phevmware-tools   (the release tag of this package)
     with the toolchain images and build tools in https://github.com/Phorepoc/toolchain and https://github.com/Phorepoc/pdk.
  2. The protocols the service implements are published as source by open-vm-tools (https://github.com/vmware/open-vm-tools);
     no open-vm-tools file is part of this package.

You may modify the package and relink it with your own build of the service: the source above is all that is needed.

https://github.com/Phorepoc and we will send you the corresponding source on a medium of your choice for the cost of the medium.
